FAQ

Straight answers before you go solar

Everything you need to know about rooftop solar in Pune.

Most homeowners reduce their electricity bills by 80% to 90%. By generating your own power during the day and banking excess units with MSEDCL via net metering, you only pay the minimum fixed connection charges.
Under the central government's PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana, residential systems receive up to ₹78,000 direct bank transfer (DBT). We register your system on the national portal and submit all documentation.
A typical 3 kW solar system requires approximately 250 to 300 square feet of shadow-free rooftop area. We design custom elevated structures so your terrace remains completely usable below.
Standard grid-tied systems automatically shut off during grid outages for lineman safety. If you need uninterrupted power during load-shedding, we also install hybrid solar systems with smart battery storage.
Solar panels convert daylight radiation, not direct heat. On cloudy or rainy days in Pune, systems typically generate 35% to 50% of peak output, and your home seamlessly draws any extra units needed from MSEDCL.
Physical rooftop installation takes 2 to 4 days. The complete cycle including MSEDCL technical sanction, meter testing, and bi-directional net meter commissioning takes about 3 to 4 weeks.
Solar panels have no moving parts. Simply washing dust off the panels with plain water every 2 to 3 weeks keeps output optimal. All Waaree modules carry a 25-year performance warranty.
